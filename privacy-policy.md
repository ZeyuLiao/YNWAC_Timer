# Privacy Policy for YNWAC Timer

Effective date: May 21, 2026

YNWAC Timer does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The extension runs only on `ynwac.com` and `www.ynwac.com`. It reads a specific page text label, "停止", only to determine whether to show, stop, and position a local timer bubble.

All timer behavior happens locally in the user's browser. No website content, browsing history, account information, or user activity is sent to any server.

YNWAC Timer does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, remote code, or third-party data services.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer.
